    What is EstudiPro?

    EstudiPro is an online platform designed for people who want to learn, organize and improve their study habits in a simple way.

    EstudiPro's philosophy combines three main pillars: organization, productivity and continuous learning. The goal is not only to offer tools to study better, but also to provide a digital environment that helps maintain consistency and order.

    Among its main features, the study timer stands out, which allows you to set a study or work time goal. In this way, the user can concentrate better and avoid distraction. It also incorporates a history section, which shows the time spent, the regularity and the evolution of habits over the days or weeks.

    Another important aspect is the possibility of customizing the experience: from choosing visual styles and colors, to establishing specific learning goals. EstudiPro wants each student to feel that the tool adapts to their own pace and way of working.



    FAQs

    Is it free?

    Currently, EstudiPro can be used for free. In the future, it may have advanced options with a subscription.

    Do I need to register to use EstudiPro?

    You cannot register for EstudiPro yet, but later you will be able to do so to save all your history in the cloud and use it whenever you want.

    Which devices are compatible?

    It works from any web browser, both on computers and mobile or tablets.

    What tools does it include?

    A study timer, statistics and aesthetic customization options.

    Is EstudiPro only for students?

    No. It is also useful for professionals, candidates, freelancers or anyone who wants to better organize their time.

    Can the appearance be customized??

    Yes. You can choose visual styles, colors and fonts so that the application adapts to your taste.

    What languages ​​are available?

    Initially it is only available in Catalan and English, but work is underway to expand to more languages ​​such as Spanish.

    What differentiates EstudiPro from other applications?

    Its combination of simplicity, basic but effective tools and a clear orientation towards constant learning.
